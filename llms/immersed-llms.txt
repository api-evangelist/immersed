# Immersed

Immersed is a spatial computing platform providing AR/VR workspaces for productivity and collaboration.

## Overview
Empowers professionals with virtual multi-monitor setups, remote telepresence, and immersive environments. Free app with optional premium features; compatible with Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro, GalaxyXR, Mac/Windows/Linux.

## Key Products & Features
- **Immersed App**: Free desktop agent; up to 5 virtual screens; solo focus mode; remote collaboration (4+ users, screen sharing, whiteboarding).
- **Visor Headset**: Lightweight 4K per eye spatial computing device (see visor.com).
- Portable, low-latency, multi-OS support.

## Target Users
Remote workers, coders, creators, teams needing focused or collaborative virtual spaces.
